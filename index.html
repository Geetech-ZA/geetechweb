Android app only. On iPhone, open Play now and use Add to Home Screen.

Install the Android app

It takes about a minute.

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, tap Settings and allow installs from this source.
  3. Tap Install, then open Geetech Drive from your home screen.

Questions

Do I need internet to play?

Yes. The game and the leaderboard load online. Without a connection you will see a parked car until you reconnect.

Will the app update itself?

Yes. The app opens the latest version of the game, so new tracks and fixes appear without reinstalling.

Why does Android show a warning?

The app is not on Google Play, so Android asks you to confirm installs from a downloaded file.
